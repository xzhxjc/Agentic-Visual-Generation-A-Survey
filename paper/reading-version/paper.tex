\abstract{
Visual generation is expanding from single-pass rendering toward visual creation processes that preserve task requirements, inspect intermediate artifacts, and revise later actions. This survey defines agentic visual generation (AVG) through observation-dependent closed-loop control, organizes the literature with six analytical components, compares systems across visual domains, reviews training and improvement methods, and examines evaluation evidence and research frontiers.
}
